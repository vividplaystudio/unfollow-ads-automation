Different question from the radar. This one ignores age and includes incumbents to ask “can this market support more than one winner?” Market depth is measured in distinct publishers — 15 publishers each with a charting app means 15 viable businesses; 15 apps from 2 publishers would just be two operators shipping clones.

The verdict is based on traction (ratings/day), not chart position. In ad-driven niches an app can be bought to real scale without ever entering a top-100 grossing chart, so “didn’t chart” is not the same as “didn’t work”. Recent entrants are found via App Store search, so they appear here even when they rank nowhere near the charts. Use the Ads link on each entrant to check whether its traction was bought.

Terms come from app names, so expect some junk — the app lists are there so you can judge.
